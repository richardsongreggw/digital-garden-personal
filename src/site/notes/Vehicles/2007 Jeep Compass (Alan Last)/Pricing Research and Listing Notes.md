Helping my brother-in-law Alan Last, research pricing for his 2007 Jeep Compass Sport before he lists it.

## Specs (from Alan via text)

2007 Jeep Compass Sport. 4 door. 4x4. 2.4L 4-cylinder.
135,720 miles.
Fair condition. Title in hand.

## Condition notes (from photos in our iMessage thread)

- Exterior paint/body look decent in side-profile shots — champagne/gold color, no obvious dents or rust visible, factory alloy wheels look fine.
- Interior is clean cloth seats, no visible tears or major staining. Automatic transmission, factory stereo/CD player, cargo area has a rubber liner.
- **Known issue**: front turn signal/headlight assembly is missing its plastic cover, exposing the amber bulb. Headlight lens also looks hazy/oxidized (common for the age but worth noting).
- Steering wheel and driver's door panel show no visible cruise control buttons or power window/lock switches — likely manual windows/locks and no cruise control, consistent with base Sport trim. **Not yet confirmed by Alan** — I asked him directly (Aug 6) and haven't heard back yet.

## Pricing research

**KBB (Sport SUV 4D, "Good" condition, typical mileage):**
- Trade-in: $600
- Private party: $1,550
- Fair Purchase Price (dealer): $1,825

**J.D. Power / NADA (formerly NADA Used Car Guide):**
- Trade-in: Low $500 / Average $1,250 / High $1,500
- Average price actually paid (dealer transactions, likely lower-mileage examples): $3,650

**KSL Cars comps (Utah/nearby, actual live listings, all dealer retail):**
- 2007 Compass Sport, 170,000 mi, Murray UT — $3,981 (dealer)
- 2008 Compass Sport, 215,606 mi, Salt Lake City — $3,999 (dealer, price reduced)
- 2011 Compass Latitude, 118,474 mi, Idaho Falls — $6,983 (higher trim/newer, less comparable)

Note: Alan's mileage (135,720) is actually lower than both directly-comparable KSL dealer listings, but his is private-party (not dealer retail) and self-rated "fair" rather than "good," plus has the headlight/turn-signal defect.

## Recommendation

- **Suggested asking price: $2,200–$2,600**
- **Likely settles around: $1,800–$2,200**

This sits meaningfully below the Utah dealer comps (private-party discount + fair condition + known defect) but above the KBB/JD Power trade-in floor, reflecting his lower mileage relative to those comps.
