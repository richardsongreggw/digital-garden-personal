## Scam prevention tips (to pass along to Alan)

- **Overpayment/fake check scam**: a "buyer" sends a cashier's check for more than the asking price and asks him to wire back the difference (for "shipping" or an "agent"). The check bounces later; he's out the wire money and possibly the Jeep.
- **Out-of-state "buyer" who can't view it in person**: often claims to be military, overseas, or sending a shipping company to pick it up. Real local buyers want to see and test-drive it.
- **Pressure to move off the platform immediately** into text/email/WhatsApp — makes it easier for them to send phishing links.
- **Fake payment app links**: messages with a link claiming to be Zelle/PayPal/an "escrow" service that's actually a phishing page.
- Only take cash in hand or a cashier's check verified by calling the issuing bank directly (not a number the buyer provides) before handing over keys/title. Insist on meeting in person. Ignore anyone offering to pay more than asking. Don't click payment links sent through chat. If someone's pushing urgency or an odd payment method, it's a scam — just stop responding.
